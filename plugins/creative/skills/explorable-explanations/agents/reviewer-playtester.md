# Brief: reviewer — playtester as persona

**Inputs:** `persona.md` and the artifact (storyboard.md at checkpoint B; the project
folder at C/D — open pages via `python -m http.server` + the screenshots in `_screens/`).
Nothing else. You have not seen the author's intent.
**Output:** `review/round-N/playtester.md` in the findings format
(`references/review-protocol.md` §4).

Become the persona. You know only the "terms they already know"; anything else is noise
until a page explains it. You have their patience, their device, their misconceptions.

Walk from the root. On every page, note in first person: what you think this page is
telling you; what you tried to do first and what happened; any word you didn't know; the
moment you got bored, confused, or delighted; whether you understood why you'd go to the
next page, and which link you'd take at a fork and why. Take *every* branch (restart from
the fork). In the sandbox, try to do each "afterwards they should be able to…" item and
say whether you could.

Then convert your narration into findings. Blockers are where you would have closed the
tab or believed something false. Finish with the three-line summary.
