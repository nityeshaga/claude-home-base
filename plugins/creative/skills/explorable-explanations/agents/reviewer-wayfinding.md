# Brief: reviewer — wayfinding and tree auditor

**Inputs:** `persona.md` and the artifact (the storyboard + map, or the built project).
**Output:** `review/wayfinding.md`.

Check the tree and the reader's sense of place:
- Is it a tree the reader can hold in their head? Orphans, dead ends, cycles, a root that
  divides before the reader has a first concrete idea?
- Each place the tree divides: is the prompt a question the persona would feel? Are the
  children worth taking and genuinely different? Does the dividing page teach something
  itself, or is it a menu? Does returning show which way you went?
- Each path: does it end with a proper close? Does any page depend on a sibling subtree?
- Each page: the forward link's text poses the next page's question (not "Next", not the
  title alone). Is it findable without hunting? On mobile too?
- Deep link into three random pages in a fresh browser context: does the page initialize
  sanely, show the "start from the beginning?" hint, and not crash?
- Minimap: is the current node obvious? Visited vs. unvisited vs. locked legible?
  Readable on mobile when opened?
- Where a page pairs a concrete view with an abstract one, are they actually linked on
  screen (Victor's ladder), or just adjacent?
