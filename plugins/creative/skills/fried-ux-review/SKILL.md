# Fried UX review

Review the UX of the app the user names using the rubric of product development and interface design that Jason Fried shared in his classic article, "The Obvious, the Easy, and the Possible".

Read the article first, before you look at the app: [references/the-obvious-the-easy-and-the-possible.md](references/the-obvious-the-easy-and-the-possible.md). It is short, and it is the whole rubric.

## The kind of app this is for

The app is very busy, intimidating and confusing to use. The navigation is confusing if not outright buggy or wrong in places, things have just been added without thinking of the hierarchy of experience, every setting is crammed on all screens and people are finding key info they care about hard to see.

If the user describes their own app's problems, their description replaces this one.

## What to produce

1. Do an adversarial review of the product UX and call out everything that is wrong or badly done or problematic. Put the findings in an HTML doc with screenshots clearly annotated with the problems so it's easy to follow.

2. Then make a video explainer with annotated screenshots of how you would restructure the UX, hide different elements and move things around to build something Jason Fried would approve of, so the user can trace every decision from before -> after with clear reasoning.

For the video's voice engine, use Google's voice model from the API.

Be detail oriented and understand the motivations of the user of the app.
